Further literature

The references attached to definitions, conventions, and constructors in the manual are the primary sources for those sections. The shorter list below points to further work that is especially relevant to two uses of the package.

Mathematical physics

Categorical Time-Reversal Symmetries develops symmetry questions for tensor categories motivated by topological phases (Wen and Schäfer-Nameki, 2026).

Arithmetic

Fusion Categories over Non-Algebraically Closed Fields develops the arithmetic theory behind splitting and scalar extension (Sanford, 2025).

Tambara–Yamagami Categories over the Reals: The Nonsplit Case studies a concrete family where the defining field changes the category (Plavnik et al., 2025).
