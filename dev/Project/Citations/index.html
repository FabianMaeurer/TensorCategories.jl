Citations

TensorCategories.jl

TensorCategories.jl and its algorithm for computing Drinfeld centers are introduced in Computing the center of a fusion category (Mäurer and Thiel, 2024). Please cite this paper when the software contributes to your research.

The mathematical algorithms and their implementation are developed systematically in Algorithmic Aspects of Tensor Categories (Mäurer, 2026).
